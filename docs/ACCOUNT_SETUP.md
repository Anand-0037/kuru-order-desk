## Registration and funding procedure

The pinned Kuru SDK describes `depositToOwner({ rootOwner, token, amount })` as the root registration/funding path. For native MON it builds AccountCore `deposit(address rootOwner, address token, uint256 amount)` and sends the same amount as transaction `value`. The first successful native deposit can register the root and put MON in Kuru custody. A **read-only** `eth_call` simulation for 0.1 MON from the selected wallet succeeded on 12 October. This is not a transaction receipt or registration proof. The approved Agent Wallet attempts did not produce a hash; do not infer onchain success or blindly resend. Keep separate MON in the wallet for gas.

For a **buy**, the AccountCore free balance must hold the deployment's exact dummy-USDC quote token, not a generic testnet USDC. The market's observed minimum notional is 10 USDC and maker fee is 4 bps; the required quote balance depends on chosen price and quantity and is computed by `prepare`. No legitimate public faucet for this exact token has been verified. For a **sell**, free native MON in AccountCore is required, and wallet MON for gas remains separate. A 0.1 MON registration deposit alone may be insufficient for the market's 10 USDC minimum at a realistic limit price.

Do not send MON to the zero-address sentinel. Deposit only to the verified AccountCore contract through a supported transaction, and do not change wallet policy or sign until the owner has reviewed the exact chain, contract, owner, amount, calldata and fee. After any confirmed deposit, rerun read-only `mm kuru prepare --official-rpc` with the owner's chosen side, price and quantity; verify the account, market, funding, maker fee, expiry and full review digest before considering any order.
